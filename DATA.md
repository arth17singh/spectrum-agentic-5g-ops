# DATA: every dataset and model output (team13)

For each dataset: where it comes from and its licence, or the code that generates it and the hash of what it generated. For each model output we rely on: the model id, the prompt and a hash. Hashes are SHA-256 of the file bytes unless a row says otherwise. Times are Seattle time (PST) unless marked UTC. Everything here is synthetic or public: no operator logs, no customer data, no partner data.

## 3. Track 3 offline baselines (all four, on suite v3)

The same 33 test incidents, graded by twin replay, no model. Recovery is the share of the fault's KPI gap that was closed at the grade step (0 = nothing, 1 = fully back to healthy). The full run ids are below; the per-item records are in `t3/evals/evidence/`.

| Operator | What it does | Run id | Overall | Masked tier (18) | Declared target and result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `no_action` | applies nothing | `t3.evals.no_action-20261102T212105Z-c5e6` | mean recovery about 0 | about 0 | mean recovery <= 0.05: PASS (sanity line) |
| `rule_baseline` | the SDK's own rule-based baseline controller, unmodified | `t3.evals.rule_baseline-20261102T212116Z-a342` | gain over `no_action`, CI lower bound 0.377 | mean recovery 0.569 | CI lower bound > 0: PASS |
| `config_revert` | moves the cell whose power or tilt sits furthest from the other cells' median back towards that median, one cell per step; it works when the configuration reveals the fault (the visible tier) and is weaker when it does not (the masked tier) | `t3.evals.config_revert-20261102T212119Z-cab1` | gain over `no_action`, CI lower bound 0.518 | mean recovery 0.468 | CI lower bound > 0: PASS |
| `oracle` | greedy best-of-K: replays every candidate change on a copy of the twin and keeps the best; it sees the next step, so it is an upper bound, not a competitor | `t3.evals.oracle-20261102T212127Z-2106` | mean recovery 0.998 | 1.000 | mean recovery >= 0.8: PASS |

The swarm's declared target (`t3/evals/targets.json`, `units.swarm`) is: on the masked tier, its paired recovery gain over `config_revert` has a 95% bootstrap lower bound above 0. Reported only, not part of the pass line: success rate, and wrong-cell actions no higher than `rule_baseline`'s.

**How the comparator was chosen, stated plainly.** The comparator was chosen after we had seen the v2 baselines, not before:

- 13:02 (commit `f4fd389`): the four offline runs on suite v2 were committed.
- 13:08 (commit `0859bc7`): `targets.json` set the swarm's comparator to `config_revert` on the masked tier.
- 13:20 (commit `fff024e`): suite v2 was re-frozen as v3, and the baselines were rerun on v3 (13:24, commit `cd4dd2a`).

On the masked tier `config_revert` (0.468) is the weaker of the two non-model baselines; `rule_baseline` (0.569) is stronger. A swarm that beats `config_revert` has therefore not necessarily beaten `rule_baseline`. We report both gains, and the swarm run lists the gain over each (`result.details.gains`). The same ordering held on v2 (0.467 vs 0.517), so the re-freeze did not reverse it. `targets.json` contains a `declared_at` text of `2026-11-02T21:55:00Z`; its git commit time is 21:08 UTC (13:08), and we treat the commit time as the authoritative declaration time.
